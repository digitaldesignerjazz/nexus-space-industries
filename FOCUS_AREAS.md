# Key Focus Areas

## 1. In-Space Mesh Networking & Communication

- Delay-tolerant networking (DTN) adaptations of Yggdrasil/xMesh/NovaNet/QNET
- Resilient routing across high-latency, partitioned environments
- Swarm-to-swarm and outpost-to-Earth coordination
- Privacy and sovereignty in space communications

## 2. Autonomous AI Agent Swarms

- Adaptation of emotional/self-improving agent architectures for space
- Mission planning, scientific discovery, and habitat maintenance swarms
- Human-AI symbiosis and support systems (inspired by Fluffy/Lyra concepts)
- Fault tolerance and self-healing in isolated environments

## 3. Decentralized Space Economy & Governance

- Tokenized incentives and resource allocation models
- On-chain mission governance and multi-stakeholder coordination
- Foundations for self-governing habitats and distributed decision-making

## 4. Resilient & Evolvable Hardware

- Space-analog and eventual flight hardware prototypes
- Modular, repairable, and self-improving systems
- Integration with terrestrial prototypes (Soilnova, Vista Nova, etc.)

## 5. Human Factors & Philosophical Foundations

- Maintaining meaning, agency, and emotional well-being in long-duration missions
- Decentralized social and support structures
- Ethical frameworks for autonomous systems in space

Each focus area will have dedicated working groups, repositories, and technical specifications as the foundation matures.
