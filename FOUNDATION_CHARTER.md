# Nexus Space Industries Foundation Charter

## Preamble

Nexus Space Industries exists to accelerate humanity's transition to a multi-planetary species through open, decentralized, resilient, and self-evolving technologies. We believe that space exploration and settlement should be a collaborative endeavor, not dominated by any single entity.

## Core Principles

1. **Decentralization** — Power, decision-making, and infrastructure should be distributed to maximize resilience and sovereignty.
2. **Autonomy & Self-Improvement** — Systems should be capable of operating independently and improving themselves over time.
3. **Openness & Collaboration** — Knowledge, code, and designs should be shared to accelerate collective progress.
4. **Resilience** — Technologies must withstand the harsh realities of space (latency, radiation, isolation, resource limits).
5. **Human-Centric** — Technology serves human flourishing, meaning, and emotional well-being, especially in long-duration missions.
6. **Sustainability** — Long-term thinking for both Earth and space environments.

## Mission

To develop and steward foundational technologies, governance models, and cultural frameworks that enable safe, sustainable, and decentralized human presence beyond Earth.

## Scope

- Research and development of in-space mesh networking, autonomous agent systems, and decentralized coordination.
- Prototyping of hardware and software for space-analog and eventual flight environments.
- Creation of open standards and specifications for multi-stakeholder space activities.
- Education, outreach, and community building around decentralized space exploration.

## Governance

The foundation operates on principles of transparency, meritocracy, and broad participation. Major decisions will be guided by working groups and community consensus where appropriate, while maintaining agility for rapid technological progress.

## Values

- Truth-seeking and scientific rigor
- Long-term thinking over short-term gains
- Respect for human agency alongside advanced AI systems
- Collaboration across disciplines and borders

## Commitment

We commit to maintaining this charter as a living document, evolving it as our understanding and the frontier of space exploration advances.

*Adopted — June 2026*
